# 🌍 Local Hire

A digital platform that connects verified local laborers with clients seeking trustworthy, skill-based services — ensuring fair wages, safety, accountability, and government oversight.

---

## ✅ Project Abstract

Local Hire is a platform designed to solve the long-standing employment gap in India’s informal labor sector. It connects workers such as farmhands, carpenters, coconut pluckers, daily-wage helpers, and part-time job seekers with clients who need timely and trustworthy services. The system ensures transparency through digital verification, wage negotiation, ratings, and document-based authentication. Government and admin portals enable regulation, reduce exploitation, and prevent child labor. The platform is scalable, sustainable, and aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals.

---

## ✅ Project Overview

In many regions of India, laborers rely on unorganized methods like roadside waiting and word-of-mouth to find work. Clients face equal difficulty in locating trustworthy workers quickly. Local Hire digitizes this system by enabling workers and clients to connect through a secure, transparent, and monitored portal. The platform includes verification processes, risk-based job approvals, a bidding/negotiation model, student-friendly part-time opportunities, and administrative monitoring.

---

## ✅ Problem Statement

The informal labor market in India lacks:

- Structured hiring mechanisms
- Worker verification and safety checks
- Fair and transparent wage systems
- Digital records or accountability
- Protection against child labor
- Accessible part-time jobs for students
- Government visibility and monitoring

This leads to unemployment, exploitation, wage inequality, and inefficiency for both clients and workers.

---

## ✅ Our Solution

Local Hire offers a technology-driven marketplace that bridges laborers and clients efficiently. Workers register with ID, age proof, address proof, and skill certificates verified by an admin or local authority. Clients can post or search for jobs based on category, location, rating, wage, and risk level. A transparent wage negotiation system ensures fair compensation for both parties. High-risk jobs require admin approval, and student jobs are regulated with safety guidelines. Every user has a maintained job history to ensure trust and accountability.
